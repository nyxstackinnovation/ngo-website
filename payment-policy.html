Payment Policy

Understanding our donation and payment processes, accepted methods, and financial transparency.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 Last Updated: January 1, 2024

This Payment Policy outlines the accepted methods, procedures, and terms related to donations and membership payments made to Sahayata Foundation.

1. Accepted Payment Methods

We currently accept donations and membership fees through the following methods:

  • UPI (Unified Payments Interface): Scan our official QR code or use our verified UPI ID through any UPI-enabled app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.)
  • Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS): Direct transfer to our official bank account using the details provided on our Donate page
⚠️
Caution: Please verify the UPI ID and bank details on our official website before making any payment. Sahayata Foundation will never ask for donations via personal phone calls, WhatsApp messages, or unofficial channels.

2. Donation Processing

  • All donations are processed directly through UPI or bank transfer. We do not use any third-party payment gateway.
  • Donations are not automatically verified by our system. After making a payment, donors are encouraged to email their payment confirmation/screenshot to donate@sahayatafoundation.org.
  • Upon verification, we will issue a donation receipt and 80G tax exemption certificate (if applicable) within 7–10 working days.

3. Minimum Donation Amount

While there is no minimum amount required, we recommend a minimum donation of ₹100 to ensure efficient processing and allocation.

4. Campaign-Specific Donations

  • When donating to a specific campaign (Education, Food, Healthcare, Women Empowerment), your contribution will be allocated to that particular program.
  • In the rare event that a campaign reaches its funding goal or is discontinued, excess funds will be redirected to the most similar active campaign, or used for the general mission. Donors will be notified in such cases.

5. General Donations

General donations are allocated at the organization's discretion to areas of greatest need, including emergency relief, operational costs, and scaling existing programs.

6. Membership Fees

  • General Membership: Free of charge
  • Active Membership: ₹500 per year
  • Patron Membership: ₹2,000 per year

Membership fees are payable via UPI or bank transfer after form submission. Your membership is activated upon payment verification.

7. Tax Benefits

Sahayata Foundation is registered under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act. All eligible donations qualify for tax deduction. To receive your 80G certificate, please email your payment confirmation to donate@sahayatafoundation.org with the following details:

  • Full name (as per PAN card)
  • PAN number
  • Donation amount and date
  • Payment reference/transaction ID

8. Currency

All donations and fees are accepted in Indian Rupees (INR) only. We do not currently accept international payments.

9. Financial Transparency

We are committed to complete financial transparency. Annual financial reports and audit statements are available upon request. Donors may write to finance@sahayatafoundation.org for details.
