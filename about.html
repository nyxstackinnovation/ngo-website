About Sahayata Foundation

Since 2015, we've been on a mission to empower India's most underserved communities through sustainable education, nutrition, and healthcare initiatives.

📖 Our Story

From a Small Idea to a National Movement

Sahayata Foundation was born in 2015 from a simple belief: every person, regardless of their background, deserves access to education, food, and healthcare. What started as a small group of volunteers in a single village has grown into a movement spanning multiple states across India.

Our founders witnessed first-hand the devastating effects of poverty, malnutrition, and lack of education in rural communities. Rather than looking away, they chose to act — and Sahayata Foundation was created to channel that action into lasting, measurable impact.

Today, we operate in over 200 villages, work with thousands of volunteers, and have positively impacted the lives of tens of thousands of children, women, and families across India.

Our Mission

To empower underserved communities across India by providing access to quality education, nutritious food, and essential healthcare services — enabling every individual to reach their full potential and lead a life of dignity.

🔭

Our Vision

An India where no child goes hungry or uneducated, where every woman is empowered, and where communities thrive through sustainable development and equal opportunity — regardless of geography or economic background.
