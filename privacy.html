Privacy & Security Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 Last Updated: January 1, 2024

Sahayata Foundation ("we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide

  • Donation Information: Name, email, phone number (when voluntarily shared via email along with payment confirmation)
  • Volunteer Registration: Name, email, phone, age, city, state, skills, and motivation
  • Membership Registration: Name, email, phone, date of birth, address, PIN code, occupation, membership tier
  • Contact Inquiries: Any information you provide when contacting us via email or phone

b) Information Collected Automatically

  • Browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • IP address (anonymized for analytics)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process volunteer and membership applications
  • To issue donation receipts and 80G tax certificates
  • To send updates about our programs and impact (with your consent)
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Data Protection & Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption on our website
  • Limited access to personal data (only authorized personnel)
  • Regular review of data handling practices
  • No storage of payment/banking details on our servers
🔒
Important: We do not process or store any payment card details or UPI credentials. All payments are handled directly through your bank/UPI app.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data only in the following cases:

  • With trusted service providers who assist in website operations (e.g., form hosting services), under strict confidentiality agreements
  • When required by law or regulatory authorities
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Sahayata Foundation

5. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies to ensure proper functioning. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. Analytics, if used, are privacy-respecting and anonymized.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at privacy@sahayatafoundation.org.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Volunteer and member records are retained for the duration of active engagement plus 3 years.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us for immediate deletion.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
