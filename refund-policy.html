Refund & Cancellation Policy

Our policy on refunds for donations and membership fees.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 Last Updated: January 1, 2024

This Refund & Cancellation Policy applies to all donations and membership fees made to Sahayata Foundation through our website or other official channels.

1. Donations — General Policy

All donations made to Sahayata Foundation are considered voluntary contributions. As a general policy, donations are non-refundable once received, as funds are immediately allocated to our ongoing programs and campaigns.

2. Exceptions — When Refunds Are Considered

We understand that errors can occur. Refunds may be considered in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • Duplicate Payment: If you accidentally made the same payment twice
  • Incorrect Amount: If you were charged an amount significantly different from what you intended (e.g., ₹10,000 instead of ₹1,000)
  • Unauthorized Transaction: If you believe a payment was made without your authorization
  • Technical Error: If a payment was made due to a technical glitch
ℹ️
Note: Refund requests must be made within 7 days of the original transaction date. Requests made after this period may not be entertained.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email us at refund@sahayatafoundation.org with the following details:

  1. Your full name
  2. Email address used during donation
  3. Transaction ID / UTR number
  4. Date and amount of the transaction
  5. Reason for the refund request
  6. Screenshot of the payment confirmation

4. Refund Processing

  • All refund requests are reviewed manually by our finance team.
  • We will acknowledge your request within 3 working days.
  • If approved, the refund will be processed within 10–15 working days from the date of approval.
  • Refunds will be made to the original payment source (same bank account or UPI ID from which the payment was made).
  • We will notify you via email once the refund has been processed.

5. Membership Fee Refunds

  • General Membership is free and involves no payment.
  • Active and Patron Membership fees are generally non-refundable once membership is activated.
  • Refunds for membership fees may be considered if requested within 7 days of payment, provided the membership benefits have not been utilized.
  • To cancel your membership, email membership@sahayatafoundation.org.

6. Campaign Cancellation

In the rare event that a campaign is cancelled or its goal is significantly altered after your donation:

  • Donors will be notified via email about the change
  • Funds will be redirected to the most similar active campaign or the general fund
  • Donors may request a refund within 15 days of the cancellation notification

7. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Change of mind after making a voluntary donation
  • Requests made after 7 days of the transaction date
  • If the donation receipt / 80G certificate has already been issued and claimed
  • If funds have already been fully allocated to a project

8. Dispute Resolution

If you are not satisfied with our response to your refund request, you may escalate the matter to our Treasurer at treasurer@sahayatafoundation.org. We commit to resolving all disputes amicably and within 30 days.
